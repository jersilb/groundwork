# Brand/Name Research — "Groundwork"
**Date**: 2026-07-31
**Prepared by**: Claude (Sonnet 5), via a 105-agent deep-research workflow (5 search angles, adversarial claim verification, 22 sources fetched, 62 claims extracted, 25 verified)
**For**: Jeremy Silbernagel, before deciding whether to proceed with formal trademark registration on "Groundwork"

**This is preliminary web research, not a legal opinion.** No full USPTO TESS search, no state registers, no international registries, no attorney review. Treat this as "is it worth paying a trademark attorney to look closer," not as clearance.

---

## Bottom line

"Groundwork" is a crowded, largely descriptive term that's already in active use across **exactly** the niches this product occupies: nonprofit/faith-based strategic consulting, AI-branded SaaS, and church/ministry group software. No single existing user is dominant enough to make the name instantly recognizable as one brand — but the sheer density of prior, active, same-category users makes this read as a **legally weak mark** and a **practically confusable name**. My recommendation: treat "Groundwork" as a placeholder, get a real attorney clearance search before investing further in the brand, and seriously consider alternatives in parallel rather than waiting on that search to finish.

## What's already using this name, closest collisions first

**1. A firm that explicitly serves faith-based organizations, doing strategic consulting** — [groundworkfirm.com](https://groundworkfirm.com/), led by Rik Rambo, lists "Faith-based organizations" as a served sector on its own site. This is the single closest collision found: same name, same target market, same general service category (strategic planning/consulting).

**2. A near-identical name already in church small-group software** — [Groundworks Ministries](https://www.groundworksministries.com/) (plural, GuideStar-listed nonprofit, Germantown TN) runs a live **Groundworks Groups App** for Bible study small groups. One letter different from your name, same vertical (church tech), an actual shipped app. Separately, "Groundwork Bible Study" (ReFrame Ministries, groundworkonline.com) uses the exact unmodified singular word in the same space.

**3. Four more nonprofit/mission-driven strategic consulting firms**, none faith-specific but all doing the same core service:
- [Groundwork Consulting](https://www.groundworkconsulting.com/) (Oregon)
- Groundwork Strategies / Ground Work Strategies LLC ([gws-va.com](https://www.gws-va.com/about), Winchester VA, ~30 years in nonprofit development)
- [Groundwork Social Sector Consulting](https://www.groundworkconsulting.org/ourwork) (San Jose, org development for youth-serving nonprofits)
- [Groundwork Strategy](https://groundwork-consulting.com/about/) (founded 2016, retreat facilitation + strategic consulting)

Five independent firms choosing this exact name for this exact service is itself evidence the word reads as descriptive rather than distinctive — which matters for trademark strength, not just naming collision.

**4. Two AI-branded companies using the name**, one conceptually close to this product:
- [thegroundwork.ai](https://www.thegroundwork.ai/) — "AI-Powered Strategic Intelligence & Transformation," enterprise clients, messaging built around "strategic planning" and "intent." Closest conceptual match found in the AI space, though its clients are general enterprise, not faith/nonprofit.
- [groundwork.ai](https://groundwork.ai/) — Groundwork AI LLC, a Brooklyn geotechnical-engineering AI/ML analytics company. Different vertical, but same "Groundwork + AI" naming pattern, and the domain is taken.

**5. A funded SaaS company operating under the exact unmodified name**: [hellogroundwork.com](https://www.hellogroundwork.com/) — "Groundwork," a video-based sales/lead-qualification platform for contractors, raised $1M pre-seed. Different industry, but a live, funded, identically-named SaaS product.

**6. A UK software company**: Groundwork Apps Ltd (Harrogate, incorporated 2023) builds Salesforce messaging-integration products — another live "Groundwork + modifier" software brand, reinforcing that this naming pattern gets chosen repeatedly and independently.

## Trademark-specific findings

- **One prior USPTO word-mark registration for GROUNDWORK exists** (Serial 86062199, Reg. 4521307, Groundwork Renewables Inc, Monterey CA) — but it's **cancelled and un-revivable** (failed Section 8 filing, cancelled Dec 2020), and was for meteorological instruments, an unrelated field. Not a live blocking registration, but not nothing either — it shows the term has been formally registered before.
- No full USPTO TESS search was run (out of scope for web research) — a real attorney search could surface live marks not found here, in Class 042 (software/SaaS) or Class 035/036 (business consulting), which are the classes that matter most for this product.

## Domain status

- **groundwork.com**: registered since 1998 to Groundwork UK (a real, long-running federation of environmental/community-regeneration charities founded 1981), current through 2027. Not available to simply register — would require a negotiated purchase, if even possible.
- **groundwork.ai**, **hellogroundwork.com**: both confirmed live and in commercial use (see above).
- **groundwork.io, groundwork.app, groundwork.co**, and close variants (groundworkapp.com, usegroundwork.com, groundworkplanning.com): **not directly confirmed either way** — this needs a direct registrar lookup, not covered by this research pass.

## My recommendation

Given the volume of prior, active, same-category and adjacent-category users — especially the firm that already explicitly serves faith-based organizations under this exact name, and the near-identical church small-group app — I'd treat this as real collision risk, not a false alarm. Two things I'd suggest, not mutually exclusive:

1. **Get an actual attorney-conducted trademark clearance search** before any further brand investment (domain purchase attempts, App Store listing, marketing). This research is a reason to prioritize that step, not a substitute for it.
2. **Start brainstorming alternative names in parallel**, rather than waiting on the legal search to come back before considering options. Given how crowded "Groundwork" already is in this specific niche, a distinctive alternative might save time and money versus trying to carve out room next to five+ existing users.
